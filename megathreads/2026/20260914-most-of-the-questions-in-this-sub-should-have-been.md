# Most of the questions in this sub should have been answered by your hermes.

**Posted by u/The1KrisRoB on 2026-09-14 · 33 points (85% upvoted) · 18 comments**

This is just a post to encourage people to make better use of this super brain you have access to. If you're using even a mildly competent model, your hermes agent is going to be able to answer questions (even about itself) much better than most people in this sub.
Being old I remember when google first launched, and the frustration I felt when people wouldn't look things up for themselves, it was like they weren't sure what they should search for. Now I'm seeing the same thing with AI.
You don't need to know the right prompt, or how to talk to your agent, just do it. You will surprise yourself with all the things you can do now.
Eg I just loaded Omarchy Linux onto my laptop, and the fingerprint reader wouldn't work. I ask hermes to fix it, and using GPT-6 it found the windows driver and reverse engineered it to create a linux driver.
You can just do things.
So go and do stuff. Be Creative. You have a tool in your hands that we could never have dreamed about 10 years ago.
