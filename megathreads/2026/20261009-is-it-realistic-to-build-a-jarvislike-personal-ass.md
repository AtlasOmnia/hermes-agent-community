# Is it realistic to build a JARVIS-like personal assistant with Hermes Agent?

**Posted by u/Bitbanga on 2026-10-09 · 77 points (93% upvoted) · 77 comments**

Hey everyone,
My ultimate goal is to build something like JARVIS from Iron Man. A personal AI assistant that knows pretty much everything about my business, projects, and personal life and can actively help me manage all of it.
Here's what I'd ideally want it to do:
Business:
Understand my company, clients, finances, invoices, and contracts. Help with invoicing, writing quotes, client acquisition, and business decisions.
Finances & Taxes:
Keep track of my financial situation, organize financial documents, help with bookkeeping, prepare tax returns, and handle other tax-related tasks.
Email & Calendar:
Read, write, and reply to emails. Manage appointments and my calendar.
Projects & Development:
Know all my projects, understand their context, write code, implement features, and work on tasks autonomously.
Research & Planning:
Research topics, brainstorm ideas, plan projects, and help me make decisions.
Personal Assistant:
Remember important information about me, my goals, preferences, and ongoing activities.
Basically, I want one central assistant that has access to all relevant information and can coordinate everything, rather than having a bunch of disconnected AI tools.
Now I'm wondering:
Is this actually realistic with Hermes Agent? How close can we get to something like this today?
Does it even make sense to put everything into one assistant? Or would it be better to use multiple specialized agents?
How would you structure such a setup? One profile with multiple projects? Sub-agents? A shared memory system?
Has anyone here built something similar? I'd love to hear about your setup and experiences.
I'd really appreciate hearing how others are approaching this.
