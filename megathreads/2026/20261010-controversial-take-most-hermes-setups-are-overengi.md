# Controversial take: most Hermes setups are over-engineered for what people actually need

**Posted by u/SnooDingos9560 on 2026-10-10 · 11 points (100% upvoted) · 11 comments**

Been lurking a lot of the showcase posts here and noticing a pattern, elaborate multi-agent societies, five subagents, cron jobs calling cron jobs. Meanwhile my own setup is basically one agent and two scheduled tasks and it covers 90% of what I actually needed automated.
Not knocking the complex setups, genuinely curious if they’re solving a real problem at that scale or if it’s more interesting to build than the simple version. Where’s the line for you between “this complexity earned its keep” and “this was fun to build but a simpler version would’ve worked just as well”?
