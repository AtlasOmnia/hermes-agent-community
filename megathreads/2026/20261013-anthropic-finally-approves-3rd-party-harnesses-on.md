# Anthropic finally approves 3rd party harnesses on Claude Sub

**Posted by u/positiveconstraint on 2026-10-13 · 121 points (88% upvoted) · 34 comments**

You CAN now use your Claude subs in third-party harnesses without paying extra.
Works with:
Claude Agents SDK
claude -p
Claude API
Claude Managed Agents
Using Claude Sub in 3rd party harnesses such as Hermes and Pi was considered a grey area, with some people afraid that doing so would lead to an account ban. Many rightfully demanded confirmation from Anthropic.
I was under the impression that Anthropic needs those harnesses more, and that it's just a matter of time.
Now this -
Update October 7, 2026:
Claude Max and Team plans now include
monthly API credits
, which cover the Claude Agent SDK,
claude -p
, the Claude API, and Claude Managed Agents. You can still use the Claude Agent SDK,
claude -p
, and third-party apps with your subscription limits.
Source:
https://support.claude.com/en/articles/15036540-use-the-claude-agent-sdk-with-your-claude-plan
They cleared the confusion on HN:
Sorry that that help center article was misleading; we’ve updated it to clarify that `claude -p` has not been removed from subscriptions as part of this change!
Source:
https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49997143
