# Turns out "just play around with it" turned into a real business

**Posted by u/isabasu on 2026-07-14 · 318 points (97% upvoted) · 122 comments**

Ok so this is a bit of a confession post. 

I'm in IT, I knew about OpenClaw and Hermes Agent from day one, and I just... didn't bother installing either for months. Classic "I don't need another tool" brain. Huge mistake in hindsight.

Backstory: 

I ran my own consulting company for a bit more than a year. It was doing genuinely well, but I quit anyway. Didn't want another 9-5 (even my own), and I kept seeing the potential in AI-driven passive income, so I decided to relax a bit at first, doing what IT nerds are doing (tinkering with software and hardware) and thats when I thought I will give Hermes a try.

Started just messing around with Hermes to get a feel for it. Then had the idea: what if I let it find local businesses with terrible or no websites? Genuinely didn't expect much. 

It crushed it - surfaced businesses from corners of the web I didn't even know existed, way better than I could've found manually digging through directories myself. 

Heads up though, your region is going to look completely different than mine, so there's no copy-paste playbook here, you'll need to let it explore your own local market.

From there it dumps everything straight into my CRM as leads, and I go through and inspect what it gathered. Then I hand-pick the ones with real potential and have Opus 4.8 (via OpenRouter) spin up a static site for each one, tailored to what Hermes actually found about that business. 

Been eyeing Sol or Fable next to get something a bit more unique looking instead of everyone's sites blending together.

Once a site's done, I launch it on a temp domain and reach out to the business - basically "hey, took the liberty of building you a better version of your site, take a look." That's it. That's the whole pitch.

And they're converting. Really well, at least in my market.

Just wanted to drop a genuine thank-you and success story here. I'm glad I gave it a go and it's been worth every bit of the OpenAI sub cost. If you're on the fence like I was - just install it already.
