# Deepseek v4.1 bad experience

**Posted by u/Present_Resource4022 on 2026-09-22 · 5 points (77% upvoted) · 6 comments**

DeepSeek V4.1 flash is shit at computer use,
I tried DeepSeek V4.1 through OpenRouter mainly for coding and It was pretty great, comparable to Sol at most of what I did with it, Twice as fast and 20x cheaper. So I plugged it into Hermes to use it for with computer work and offloading it stuff I normally want an agent to handle, mainly PDFs, Word, Excel and moving information between apps. And repetitive high quantity work
The model understands what I want, but controlling the computer is where it’s underperforming . It misses clicks, types in the wrong places, skips parts of documents while scrolling and gets confused about what is selected. It also spends too much time thinking about basic cursor actions and doesn’t recover well when it makes a mistake.
I didn’t give up on it yet , wrote skills for it , wasted an entire day just to train it on what I wanted and how to work and approach, every single scenario it got wrong I gave it feedback but it’s still underperforming even against my locally run Qwen3.8-27b
I ended up switching to Terra at high reasoning through ChatGPT subscription and it’s been much more reliable for me at computer use and its results on my workflow, The benchmarks favor Deepseek v4.1 but it can’t do moderate level work .
Looking forward to the upcoming GPT 6 Luna/Terra/Sol models next week 🤙
