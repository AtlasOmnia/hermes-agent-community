# TIL: You don’t need multiple profiles to multi-thread conversations with your AI agent—just use Telegram Topics!

**Posted by u/Leather_Elephant7281 on 2026-10-08 · 7 points (88% upvoted) · 4 comments**

I feel super dumb for not figuring this out earlier.
I was actually creating different profiles just so I could have multiple ongoing conversations with my Hermes agent at the same time. Juggling accounts just to run parallel chats with one AI was way too much work.
Then the lightbulb finally went off: Telegram Topics.
Now, my setup is super simple:
Create one group.
Turn on Topics.
Start a new topic whenever I want a new conversation thread with Hermes.
Boom. Easy multi-threading in a single place. No account switching, no context clashing, and my sanity is restored.
If you’ve been jumping through hoops to multitask with your AI agent, save yourself the headache and just use topics.
