# Any way to make Hermes more "proactive"?

**Posted by u/ozone6587 on 2026-10-03 · 68 points (93% upvoted) · 43 comments**

I am comparing my Hermes agent with the competition at the moment. So Grok Bot and Muse and OpenAI's "Dots" and I notice that Hermes is not very proactive or "agentic" at all in comparison. I know this is open source so "competition" is a loaded word but you get what I mean.
I've been using it to manage my homelab and it works great but I have to initiate everything. I have setup cronjobs to routinely send me my schedule, read emails and all that but I just get summaries and it rarely acts on anything.
It's good for reports but I never get pinged to act on certain items and it never makes "recommendations" or proactively move my calendar event if something gets canceled.
Basically, it feels more like chat with "agentic tasks" on requests instead of acting like a personal agent. Any tips?
