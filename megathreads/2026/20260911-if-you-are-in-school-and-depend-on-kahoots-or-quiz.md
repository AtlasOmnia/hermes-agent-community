# If you are in school and depend on kahoots or quizlets, this is the use case for you.

**Posted by u/Ok_Television8309 on 2026-09-11 · 8 points (100% upvoted) · 2 comments**

Hi all, I’m a high schooler using Hermes agent, and just yesterday I came across an awesome use case. I had a study guide pdf from one of my classes, and I struggle to study the old fashioned way in study guides. Normally I depend on a kahoot but I did not feel like making one and didn’t have much time to do so. So I tried something else. I sent the pdf to my agent and had it build a GitHub pages site with a quizlet like interface for practicing the questions in the study guide. It works great! This use case is a heavy productivity booster.
