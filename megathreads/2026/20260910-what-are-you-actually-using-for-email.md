# What are you actually using for email?

**Posted by u/Soft-Lie-434 on 2026-09-10 · 9 points (90% upvoted) · 42 comments**

Genuine question, not rhetorical. Every time I see someone give their agent a real capability, email is where it gets awkward, and I can't tell what the common solution is.
So: does your agent touch email at all? And if it does:
- Its own inbox (AgentMail or similar), or your real mailbox?
- Read-only, or can it send?
- If it can send, what stops it from sending something wrong? Do you approve every one, or do you just trust it?
- Or did you look at it, decide it wasn't worth the risk, and leave email out entirely?
That last one is a real answer and I'd like to know how many of you picked it.
Disclosure: I maintain an email MCP server, so I have a horse in this race. But I'm asking because I don't know the answer, not to pitch and I'll keep the link out of the post.
