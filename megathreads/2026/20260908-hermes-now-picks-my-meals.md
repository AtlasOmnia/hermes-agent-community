# Hermes now picks my meals

**Posted by u/dreameruk on 2026-09-08 · 29 points (87% upvoted) · 13 comments**

I installed Hermes Agent last week and one of the first things I did, after setting up WhatsApp, was to give it access to my Ocado account.
Then, I downloaded the digital versions of all my cookbooks and saved them on the Hermes server. I asked my Hermes to make a database of the recipes and pick five for me to make and put the ingredients in the Ocado basket. I then asked it to tell me which books and page numbers the recipes come from.
I gave it a budget to stick to, which must also include delivery cost and it did it perfectly, it even booked a time slot for the food to be delivered, even though I didn’t explicitly tell it to do that.
FYI, I’m using GPT5.6 Sol as it’s AI brain and I’m on the £20 a month plan. I’ve only been using it a week, so I’m not sure if that plan will stop at some point and I’ll have to downgrade to Terra or Luna.
