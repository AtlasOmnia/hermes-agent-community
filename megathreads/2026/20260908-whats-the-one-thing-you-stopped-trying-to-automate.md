# What's the one thing you stopped trying to automate with Hermes and just do manually now?

**Posted by u/SnooDingos9560 on 2026-09-08 · 7 points (100% upvoted) · 2 comments**

Same kind of question as one I saw floating around for ChatGPT, but curious what it looks like specifically for Hermes given how much of this sub is about cron jobs, subagents, and long-running setups.
For me it's anything involving file cleanup after a subagent finishes a task — reorganizing outputs, renaming things consistently, clearing out intermediate junk. I'll get a subagent to handle it and it technically works, but then I'm double-checking the file structure anyway, so at some point I just started doing that part myself and only handing off the actual task.
Curious what other people's version of that is. Could be a cron thing, a memory/context thing, something with subagent handoffs specifically. Not trying to start a "Hermes can't do X" pile-on, more interested in where people actually draw the line between "worth automating" and "faster to just do it," since that line's probably different for everyone depending on setup.
