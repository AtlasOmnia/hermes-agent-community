# Bots get progressively worse over time

**Posted by u/ChampionDad on 2026-10-19 · 6 points (80% upvoted) · 12 comments**

I understand Hermes is the 'self-improving' harness, but I am not seeing it. In fact, the bots I've been working with for three months have been getting progressively worse.
They tend to quit tasks more. The tasks they complete generate reams of markdown of chain of thought, and decisions text inside the deliverable document. Just dumb stuff you don't see inside a Claude, Chat, etc. harness when you ask for something.
I have several bots and I've moved them all to frontier models (Claude Opus 5.5 for writing, GLM53, Codex, Opus or Grok for coding, etc.) but they still have been doing stupid things.
I have AGENT guides, SOULs, etc. and I ask them to follow certain rules early on but they tend to drift on the meaning and give up because of some skewed misunderstanding of a very clear rule early on.
I added Mnemosyne for memory expansion to see if it helped, but so far, not really.
My grand thought was I would have a team of Bots handling day-to-day shit, all via a point-bot CoS, but all I've been doing lately is correcting them, asking why shit isn't getting done, etc. Meanwhile, I'm doing it `old-school` prompting Claude Code directly and it's just pushing PRs without a fart.
My gut says these bots act like they're loading 700k of context or something for each new session, but I don't see that much token spend.
I'm hoping someone who's experienced this and fixed it can tell me what I'm doing wrong and what I should do to fix this.  💪💪
