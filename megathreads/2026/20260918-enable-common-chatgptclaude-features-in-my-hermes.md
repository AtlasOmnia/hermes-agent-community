**Posted by u/CCC911 on 2026-09-18 · 8 points (100% upvoted) · 15 comments**

When I ask ChatGPT (even the free version) for some recommended restaurants in a new city, it naturally shows me a nice interface with Maps embedded right into the view, photos, and some yelp info.

ChatGPT has a natural voice chat function.  This is great when walking or driving when I cannot read the screen or touch the device in any way.   I just talk.
