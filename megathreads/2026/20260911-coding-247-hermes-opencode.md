# Coding 24/7? Hermes + Opencode

**Posted by u/Informal_Cap_5247 on 2026-09-11 · 8 points (100% upvoted) · 7 comments**

Just sharing my experience... We've been using a hermes agent inside our AI workforce (in emperorclaw os) to build and take care of some projects we have...  When left hermes coding tasks, we encountered that many times it was coding slow, being busy doing 1 time... Or simply, it just stopped working.
So, we tried the following: added opencode and trained the agent to give the tasks to it. With a cron on top of it to check/follow the todo list and progress in openrouter.
Benefits?
- Agent wasn't busy, ready to reply to you.
- It really took ownership of managing the coding process rather that being itself the coder.
- The output was much better and the agent could keep working 24/7.
Hope this help you.
Cheers!
