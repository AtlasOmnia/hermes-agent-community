# Claude sub is arguably the best thing i've tried yet for hermes.

**Posted by u/lolfacemanboy on 2026-10-13 · 25 points (80% upvoted) · 36 comments**

Not AI slop, just voice to text that's been given a grammar polish
.
To start I'm not one of those normal max out subscription people who buys max sub and burns Opus 5.5 medium on the most mundane tasks.
And to immediately fight back against anyone that is saying that Getting a sub via the HermesagentSDK plugin is an instant ban or anything of the like. It is not. I spent two days hedging on the decision to buy the sub simply because of this.
I've looked at benchmarks, I've looked at Artificial Analysis, basically everything I can, plus my own experience with it too. Short of long term agentic use (like, making and pushing a large pr, not just a long todo list), Haiku 5.5 is practically GLM 5.3, for a price ratio GLM 5.3 never hit. Especially at the TPS.
I'd implore as many of you as possible to get a Claude sub, even a pro sub, and just run Haiku 5.5 on Max as your day to day model.
I've been running this for a couple days now. I've had a couple months using OpenCode Go. I've used APIs, short of Codex I've tried everything.
And even then I've used the GPT models through API, so I'm not blind to OpenAI's stuff. For agentic use, quickly doing tasks is great as a subagent thing, or for deterministic workflows. Not simple actions, but straightforward ones.
When OpenAI models hit longer messy stuff though, it falls apart a bit easier than Claude models. You get scope creep, weird things.
So for anyone right now spending 10 bucks on OpenCode Go, double it. It's the same as buying two OpenCode Go subs, but your usage is effectively GLM 5.3. And for anyone on API, short of DeepSeek v4.1 flash users running on like 3$ a month or some shit, just get the sub.
