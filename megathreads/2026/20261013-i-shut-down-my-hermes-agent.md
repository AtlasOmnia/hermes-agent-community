# I shut down my Hermes agent.

**Posted by u/Lucimous on 2026-10-13 · 206 points (90% upvoted) · 151 comments**

I recently shut down my Hermes agent, ARGUS, and honestly, it was a little sad.
I built him on a dedicated laptop, connected him to Discord, and spent a lot of time working on his memory, skills, and integrations.
My goal was to have something like JARVIS from Iron Man. An assistant that remembers me, understands my life, takes initiative, helps me think, and actually gets things done.
Instead, I found myself spending more time maintaining and improving him than actually benefiting from him. I felt more like his system administrator than his user.
I’m not giving up on Hermes entirely. I still use another agent, Ari, for specific work automations that genuinely save me time.
But the dream of an always-on, intelligent personal assistant just didn’t work out the way I’d imagined.
Has anyone else experienced this?
Have you managed to build an agent that’s genuinely useful in everyday life without constantly maintaining it? Or are we all spending more time building our assistants than actually using them?
