# Dear Hermes Creators: I'm so impressed with your release cadence. Please share the sauce!

**Posted by u/Lean-Anders on 2026-10-08 · 18 points (82% upvoted) · 20 comments**

I'm the creator of a non-competitive product and I of course use AI coders and I have a CI/CD pipeline and things like git-actions. But your guys release pace seems phenomenal. Of course, I'm aware of the fact that your developers are almost all certainly running multiple AI coding agents. And those coding agents are triggering, or at least I think they're triggering PRs, which probably have all sorts of automated checks, code reviews, and get thrown back when they need something fixed. But even accounting for all that automation, it's still very impressive.
You hear all the time that the learning organization is going to succeed. The organization that is constantly watching its users, their uses, their problems, the market, other products, and adapting and learning from users. And as far as I can tell, you guys are just killing it in that area.
I'm a solo developer and I literally today just onboarded my first user today! So I have a ways to go but I was pretty proud of the fact that he sent me feedback and and I got a fix out for that in a little less than an hour.
But that doesn't compare to you guys.
I want that for my product! So I thought I'd take a chance and just ask you at a high level without creating any security issues, tell us what a day in the life of the Hermes development pipeline looks like.
