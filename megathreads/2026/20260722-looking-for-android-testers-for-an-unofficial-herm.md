# Looking for Android testers for an unofficial Hermes companion app

**Posted by u/Brainyface on 2026-07-22 · 5 points (100% upvoted) · 1 comments**

I’ve been building an unofficial Android companion app for Hermes Agent and I’m looking for a small group of testers.

The app connects to an existing self-hosted Hermes installation and currently supports chat, session history, skills, memory, schedules, logs, diagnostics, configuration, and other dashboard features.

I’m especially looking for people who can test:

* Connecting over LAN, Tailscale, or another VPN
* Authentication and connection reliability
* Chat streaming and tool activity
* Different Android devices and screen sizes
* Crashes, battery usage, and general usability

A few notes:

* This is an independent project and is not affiliated with Nous Research.
* The app is currently closed source.
* It will be free during testing.
* I’m still deciding what the longer-term release model will look like.
* Early testers who provide meaningful feedback will receive complimentary access when it launches.

The app connects directly to your Hermes server. I do not receive or store your Hermes credentials or conversations.

Message me with your Android device, Android version, and how you currently access Hermes if you’re interested.
