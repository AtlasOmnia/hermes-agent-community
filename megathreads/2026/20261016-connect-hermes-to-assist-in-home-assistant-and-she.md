# Connect Hermes to Assist in Home Assistant, and she’s an action button push away from your iPhone. With Voice!!!

**Posted by u/Khaaaaannnn on 2026-10-16 · 8 points (100% upvoted) · 14 comments**

I’m not sure why I didn’t think of this sooner. But you can use the extended open AI conversation integration and connect Hermes to your home assistant as an voice assistant, from there, with the Home Assistant iOS app, you can map a shortcut to the action button on your iPhone and basically always have push to talk with your agent. While the assist screen’s up, if she doesn’t respond with a question and keep listening again, just hold the action button. It’s been game changing for which “I want Hermes to ___” thoughts. I pull out my phone, hit the button and boom, done.
