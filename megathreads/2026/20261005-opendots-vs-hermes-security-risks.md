# OpenDots vs Hermes (Security Risks)

**Posted by u/Material_Bell8364 on 2026-10-05 · 10 points (100% upvoted) · 6 comments**

I was thinking about testing this to see if we could help less technical users. But I noticed that you needed their .env file requires INTELLIGENCE_API_KEY. Which apparently is the account tied to them.
After some research, Claude, Hermes and I found this
This policy covers your
account and website
data: email, password, payment info, and how you use their site. It explicitly excludes data CopilotKit processes on behalf of its customers. That data is governed by CopilotKit's separate agreements with those customers. Your OpenDots conversations stored in Intelligence most likely fall into that excluded category, so this policy doesn't tell you how your chats are handled.
For the data it does cover, a few things are worth knowing:
They may use aggregated or de-identified data however they choose, including for research, modeling, and marketing.
They share data with advertising partners. Meta and Google Analytics are named.
Their website records session details, including mouse movements, clicks, and text you type.
Data may be stored in the US and other countries.
How does the rest of the community feel about this. Open dots is interesting and can replace Hermes or beware they are grabbing all of the data that they can?
