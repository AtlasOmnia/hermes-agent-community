# Can someone explain the actual advantage of Bot Mode over Profiles + long-running chats?

**Posted by u/akgo on 2026-09-26 · 63 points (98% upvoted) · 11 comments**

I’ve been using Hermes for quite a while, and before Bot Mode I already had multiple specialist Profiles — for example SEO, Strategist, Developer, etc.
For each Profile, I usually keep a dedicated long-running chat and continue talking in that same chat whenever I come back to that topic.
Now with Bot Mode, I’m trying to understand what I should actually change in my workflow.
From what I understand, a Bot is still basically a Profile underneath, but with a persistent/canonical Bot chat, routines, easier bot-to-bot communication, group chats,
u/mentions
, etc.
But this is where I’m confused:
If I already have an
SEO Profile + one long-running SEO chat
, and I keep coming back to that same chat, how is that meaningfully different from using an SEO Bot and continuously talking inside its Bot chat?
A few specific questions:
Are Bots supposed to replace the way we previously used Profiles + Chats, or are they mainly another UI/workflow on top of Profiles?
Should I stop creating separate chats and just keep talking to the same Bot indefinitely for everything related to its role?
What does a Bot actually gain over an existing Profile with one dedicated long-running chat?
Profiles were already specialized, could have their own memory/skills/config, and agents could already delegate or communicate — so
what is the major practical advantage of Bot Mode?
Is the biggest benefit basically the
multi-agent/team orchestration
—
u/mentions
, group chats, routines attached to specific Bots, easier bot-to-bot communication, etc.?
For people who have been using Hermes Profiles for months, are you
converting your existing Profiles into Bots
, or creating new Bots from scratch?
What are you still using normal
Chats/Sessions
for now that Bot Mode exists?
Is a good rule basically:
long-term specialist/role = Bot
and
temporary/one-off task = normal Session
?
I’m especially interested in hearing from people who were already heavy users of Profiles before Bot Mode launched.
I understand why Bot Mode looks cleaner from an organization/UI perspective, but I’m trying to understand the
real workflow advantage
, because right now one persistent chat inside a specialist Profile feels very similar to one persistent Bot chat.
Would love to hear how people are actually using it in practice, rather than just the feature description.
