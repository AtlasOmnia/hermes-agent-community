# I spend more guiding Hermes than getting work done

**Posted by u/edstevo on 2026-09-17 · 30 points (89% upvoted) · 21 comments**

I really like the idea of Hermes, and I can see that it could be good for a role based approach. I.e. like training a new member of staff who remembers how to do a range of tasks and remembers the quirks of how tasks need to be executed within their role. But honestly, especially when working with custom plugins to interact with my apps, I spend more time getting Hermes to actually work with these plugins (refreshing tool lists, understanding that it should use the plugin, messing around connecting and figuring out what to do) than actually getting any work done.
Anyone else here with this problem? Any advice?
I should be clear, I'm using a mix of models, mainly Qwen3.8:27b and OpenAi and Claude frontier models.
