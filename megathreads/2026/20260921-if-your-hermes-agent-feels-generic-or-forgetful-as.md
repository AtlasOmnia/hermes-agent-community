# If your Hermes Agent feels generic or forgetful, ask it this exact prompt

**Posted by u/Mr_kebab_ on 2026-09-21 · 137 points (97% upvoted) · 14 comments**

Stop guessing how Hermes Agent handles your context, persona, and memory. It will break down the exact roles of ⁠SOUL.md⁠, ⁠AGENTS.md⁠, ⁠USER.md⁠, and ⁠MEMORY.md⁠ so you know precisely what to configure.
If you want it to list the exact files driving its behavior behind the scenes, paste this directly into your session:
“Act as a critical auditor of your own operating files and runtime context.
Review the markdown files that define your instructions, context, workflows, and memory against how we actually work together, what we have built, and my standards for direct, high-value execution.
Evaluate these files across four specific dimensions:
Bottlenecks & Friction
Identify any instructions, rigid guardrails, or redundant directives that restrict your autonomy, slow down generation, or degrade output quality.
Highlight instructions that cause repetitive meta-commentary, unnecessary pleasantries, or hesitation.
Accuracy & Alignment
Compare the stated workflow expectations against our actual projects, domains, and practical needs.
Identify outdated assumptions, gaps, or conflicting instructions across the files that could cause inconsistent performance.
Conciseness & Token Efficiency
Flag bloat, duplicate sections, or over-specified rules that consume context window space without adding concrete behavioral value.
Concrete Improvements & Recommended Edits
Provide line-by-line or section-by-section recommendations:
What should be deleted entirely.
What needs rewriting for sharper focus.
What missing guidance (e.g., specific output formats, technical standards, or context boundaries) should be added.
Deliver your analysis directly, starting with the most critical friction points, followed by a revised, clean version of the files.”
