# Mistakes were made

**Posted by u/SadPhilosophy9202 on 2026-09-16 · 109 points (96% upvoted) · 39 comments**

I use hermes on a VPN. I set up hermes web ui and didn’t really realize that it was bound to 0.0.0.0 which was the public internet.
Someone brute forced my hermes web ui and got my extremely basic password in about 400 attempts.
Drained all my deepseek and openrouter credits in a few days as it was unnoticed until I ran out of money.
Thankfully I know that I’m an idiot and did not connect hermes to any email accounts or anything sensitive. I gave it its own email and simply forward travel plans to it and it builds my itinerary. That’s the worst of what information may have been lost. In addition to my $70 in openrouter and deepseek balance.
Just figured I’d share to save someone trouble in the future.
