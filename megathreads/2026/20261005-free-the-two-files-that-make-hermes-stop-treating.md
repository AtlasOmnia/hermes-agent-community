# Free: the two files that make Hermes stop treating you like a stranger (SOUL.md + USER.md pack)

**Posted by u/richi71 on 2026-10-05 · 28 points (81% upvoted) · 9 comments**

Everybody's first week with Hermes looks the same: you install it, it works, and it still doesn't know anything about you. You re-explain your job, your projects, your setup every session.
The fix is two files that get injected into the system prompt on every turn:
SOUL.md
, who the agent is: name, voice, what it may decide alone, what it must never touch.
memories/USER.md
, who you are: what you actually do all day, which tools you already use, what you hate doing, what's off limits.
I wrote a Week 1 pack for this. It's a folder, not a course:
an identity questionnaire (11 questions, ~10 minutes)
filled templates for
SOUL.md
and
USER.md
with the blanks marked
install.py
that finds your Hermes home, backs up what's already there, and drops the templates in place
test.md
with 6 checks to run in a
fresh session
, so you know it loaded on its own
No signup, no email, no upsell. MIT licensed, do whatever you want with it.
What I actually want back: run the 6 tests and tell me where it breaks. The failure I'm most curious about is the agent contradicting the file you just wrote. If that happens to you, I want to know about it.
Link in the comments.
