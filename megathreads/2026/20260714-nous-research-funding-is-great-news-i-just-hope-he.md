# Nous Research funding is great news — I just hope Hermes doesn’t lose what makes it special

**Posted by u/kafetz on 2026-07-14 · 114 points (98% upvoted) · 35 comments**

TechCrunch is reporting that Nous Research is in talks to raise funding at a $1.5 billion valuation:

[https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/13/hermes-agent-maker-nous-research-in-talks-for-new-funding-at-1-5b-valuation/](https://reddit.comhttps://techcrunch.com/2026/07/13/hermes-agent-maker-nous-research-in-talks-for-new-funding-at-1-5b-valuation/)

Seeing Nous Research attract this level of investment is genuinely exciting. It shows that Hermes is gaining serious recognition and could give the team the resources to improve it much faster.

At the same time, I really hope the funding doesn’t change their fundamental approach.

What makes Hermes appealing to me is that it is open source, self-hostable, and gives users a lot of control over how their agent operates. I hope investor pressure doesn’t gradually turn it into a cloud-first product where the most useful features are locked behind subscriptions, local deployments become secondary, or development starts focusing almost entirely on enterprise customers.

There is obviously nothing wrong with Nous building a sustainable business or offering a convenient hosted version. I just hope they continue treating the open-source, self-hosted version as a core product rather than as a limited community edition designed mainly to push users toward the cloud.

Funding could make Hermes significantly better. Hopefully, it helps Nous strengthen the project without losing the philosophy that attracted so many people to it in the first place.

Any other thoughts out there?
