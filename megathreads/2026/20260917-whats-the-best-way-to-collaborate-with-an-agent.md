# What's the best way to collaborate with an agent?

**Posted by u/Oferlaor on 2026-09-17 · 12 points (92% upvoted) · 21 comments**

What I mean is, let's say you have a remote Hermes agent that's not running on your computer (that's relatively straight forward, I think). You can communicate with it through Hermes desktop or use Slack/Whatsapp/Telegram/Discord/etc.
But most of these communication platforms are not threaded, so that means you either give the agent too much context or not enough.
Are you using a second brain or external resource so it can collaborate with other agents?
How are you sharing artifacts/presentations/HTML files/documents with the agent? Are you giving it access to Github/Google-drive/Dropbox or are you ping-ponging files from your computer back and forth to it?
