# is there a better way to make a to do list with hermes that works with speech to text, rather than android auto and setting reminders?

**Posted by u/FreezedPeachNow on 2026-09-16 · 7 points (100% upvoted) · 10 comments**

Most of ah-ha moments (ah crap I forgot to call the doctor) happen while I am driving.
Right now I have a pixel phone and android auto.  And I try to say "hey Google, set a reminder to call the Doctor at 2pm."  It sucks.  Half the time it doesnt work, it stores most of those reminders in its own manner.  There is no easy way to review everything or reorganize tasks (when not driving).
I normally use Google Keep, easier to make lists and rearrange things, etc.  But unfortunately Google Keep does not work with Android Auto.
So I figured there has to be a better way to do things with Hermes.  I have been successfully using it to do other things for the past 6 months.  Why not try to do this better.

I want the quickest way to use voice to text to communicate via the gateway.  Right now my gateway of choice is telegram.  Only because that was the easiest gateway to setup.
I know that I am not likely to get a wake-screen-hey-google level of voice to talk with any of the hermes gateway options.  You know how you can say "hey google" on a pixel from from the lock screen and then it will wake up and listen for your command?  If I could somehow that would be great! "Hey Hermes" while driving "set a reminder at 2pm to call the doctor".  But if not is there another gateway android app that could be setup to use a widget to start talking.  Like discord or whatsapp or one of the other options.  That way I am not trying to open telegram with my finger, and then open my bots conversation, then pushing the microphone button while driving.  Basically, minimizing the amount of steps I need to do with my fingers while driving to dictate a note.  So if you could just like open the phone and push a widget button and then start talking to one of the gateways, that would be ideal.
Any pointers on the best way to do this?
