# Hermes helped improve my marriage (Meal Planning)

**Posted by u/rgreen192 on 2026-10-10 · 15 points (85% upvoted) · 4 comments**

Every week, when I try to meal plan my wife (who I adore and love dearly, don’t get the wrong message here) always goes “I don’t wanna do that right now” and “I don’t care what we eat” but never wants to come back to it. So it ends up being 3+ attempts over a day or two and she finally annoyingly relents and we plan it out. Then she doesn’t like when I’m on my phone doing the pickup order.
So I had Hermes fix both of those problems for me.
I found out Kroger has an API, so I had Hermes via gpt sol 6.1 plan out access to my pickup cart.
I also recently added Mealie to my docker containers and imported all of my personal recipes, most of which were just jotted down messes in my notes. It cleaned them up, matched the ingredients to what mealie has them listed under, and scaled the ingredients 3 adult meals like I asked.
Then I asked it to suggest me x amount of recipes per week that my wife and I can approve or deny, recommend me 2 new recipes per week for us to try from some of my favorite sources, and it adds the ingredients to our pickup cart. It asked for my brand and frozen/fresh preferences to save, as well as any dietary restrictions, and gives me a list to approve, which I can add or subtract from, then adds it to the cart for me to choose a pickup time.
This is exactly the use case I was hoping for with Hermes, and with a little bit of back and forth over telegram in my free minutes throughout the day, I have something that has simplified my life by just a little bit.
And it’s wife tested and approved.
