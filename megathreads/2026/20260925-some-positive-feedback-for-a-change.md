# Some positive feedback for a change

**Posted by u/EtrainFilmz on 2026-09-25 · 21 points (95% upvoted) · 12 comments**

I’m surprised at the amount of hate I’m seeing on here. I think a large part of the hate is attributable to user error. The vast majority of complaints I see on here are from people whining that their “free” models aren’t running an agentic harness properly. Yeah, no shit. That isn’t a Hermes issue, that’s a model selection issue.
I’ve been a daily user for about 3 months now and Hermes helps run my side business. In my day job, our team has implemented Claude Cowork so I’ve experience in both harnesses. I gotta say, I dread working in Claude Cowork and it’s night and day when I’m home working on Hermes.
Today I was granted permission to use Hermes at work, connected it to the Claude SDK, and started working through it instead of Cowork. God damn, is it beautiful.
This is anecdotal and certainly my personal opinion, but hats off to Nous for a stellar, free product.
