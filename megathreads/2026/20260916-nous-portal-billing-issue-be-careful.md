# Nous Portal billing issue - be careful!

**Posted by u/alice-allison on 2026-09-16 · 10 points (100% upvoted) · 3 comments**

https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent/issues/110912
There is currently an issue where billing for models on nous portal is NOT matching what is listed on the nous portal models page - for some models, it is SIGNIFICANTLY higher (deepseek v4 flash is a major one.)
Be careful about using non-free models from the nous portal provider until this gets fixed!
There is two separate bugs here - one is for the amount that hermes displays when you query it for usage rates (visual bug, you can still ask hermes to get the upstream provider cost, which is accurate) - the other is that nous portal is billing at drastically different rates than it says it is.
