# Health, fitness and nutrition life changing

**Posted by u/seddu on 2026-09-15 · 10 points (100% upvoted) · 0 comments**

I created a health bot. I had Claude create a persona of a medical health and longevity specialist. I call her Dr Beverly. I regularly upload screenshots from my fitness watch app and it analyzes everything and provides constructive feedback. I provided it with my last two years of medical records particularly blood tests. The analysis has been nothing short of incredible and has been enormously helpful in interacting with my doctor. It reminds me to ask particular questions to my doctor. Attracts all my doctor's appointments and puts it in my Google calendar. I also use it to track my blood pressure two or three times a day. Created an app for tracking and it always provides me with feedback and insight on my blood pressure. I use it to track my meals on occasion. I just started using it to track my fitness. It created a full fitness plan for me and it added it to my Google calendar with complete instructions. It also created a daily meal plan and shopping list for healthy eating. Can't stress enough how amazing the analysis is it provided me with detailed and constructive feedback and has given me new insights on my health. I use deep seek but I occasionally cross reference information with Claude and Gemini AI.
