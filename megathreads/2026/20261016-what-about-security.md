# What about security?

**Posted by u/takoulseum on 2026-10-16 · 9 points (100% upvoted) · 11 comments**

I am a quite recent hermes user and I love it, but I am always thinking about security (and privacy).
My setup (which may not be the best solution) is the following.
At home
- hermes and inference endpoints are all hosted locally in my homelab
- hermes has full access without restriction on my homelab and manages it (which saves me a lot of time)
- the homelab is fully isolated from my home network through an opnsense firewall (hosted in a old pc with pcie expansions for ethernet ports)
In the cloud
- a vps that hosts the matrix/nginx server. The goal was to connect from my phones to hermes without having to open a port or use a tunnel, while keeping privacy
Now I am still wondering how security can be improved especially for the prompt injection issues, that will probably be more and more spread.
So my questions:
How do you consider the security / privacy aspects in your hermes life?
Is prompt injection a concern for you or not at all?
I am also curious why (consider the power of hermes agent) there is no flair for security/privacy topics, are you all confident with your setup?
