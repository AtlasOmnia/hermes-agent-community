# Why Hermes Still Beats Dots (for me)

**Posted by u/OhJohnO on 2026-10-01 · 53 points (96% upvoted) · 14 comments**

For anyone curious, my Hermes setup still beats the heck out of Dots for multiple reasons. I spent the day yesterday testing OpenAI’s new offering and here is what I found.
With Hermes, my data and my credentials live on my computer. While I still do use the frontier models for many tasks, I am not leaving my data on their servers. Big difference if you value your privacy.
Open source - I have built forks and small customizations to Hermes that work for me and my small business. I have absolutely no ability to customize Dots.
Model selection - Yes, GPT 6 Astra is excellent. But it is not the right model for every task. Much has been made about Astra being AGI. (Spoiler: it’s not.) It’s absolutely not the right tool for every job. For those of us who use it regularly, we know that it is prone to over analyzing and overthinking even simple tasks on low reasoning. Hermes allows me to select the right model for the job. I use a hammer to hit a nail, not a jackhammer.
Custom API or MCP usage - OpenAI has built Dots to refuse to use credentials that are stored, even securely, on your computer. Even when credentials are not exposed to the agent and are stored in a secure repo like 1Password and delivered via script, Dots refuses to execute. With the multiple custom API tools I am using for my business, this is an absolute non-starter. Bottom line, Dots only works with plugins listed in OpenAI’s library.
