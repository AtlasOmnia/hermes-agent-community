# Hermes is the missing puzzle piece

**Posted by u/eodgooch on 2026-07-21 · 7 points (77% upvoted) · 4 comments**

A few months ago I was burned out building Langchain agents. I picked up Hermes on the advice of a friend and it's been revolutionary. 

It has been the missing puzzle piece for me. 

Yesterday I launched a website I've been working on and off for months. Hermes has been instrumental in helping me curate data. If I'm out networking and someone tells me about a new spot in town, I open up Telegram and tell Hermes to get to work. A few minutes later I can show the person the data live on the site. They are usually blown away.

I'm waiting on some paperwork before I can go and talk with businesses in town. I am so excited to show business owners Hermes. And how to get their own intern with their chatgpt account.

I'm downplaying what I've done here. I'm running several agents. I use hermes with a local Firecrawl inside a Tailscale network. It has access to my VPS and APIs. I set up a local temp db for Hermes to "stage" data for curation. It's been awesome so far!
