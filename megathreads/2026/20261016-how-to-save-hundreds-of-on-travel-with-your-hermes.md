# How to save hundreds of $$$ on travel with your Hermes agent

**Posted by u/Efistoffeles on 2026-10-16 · 96 points (97% upvoted) · 9 comments**

If you book a hotel with free cancellation, the price can still drop after you book. Tell Hermes to set a cron job that checks the same room every morning until the free cancellation ends and sends you a Telegram message if it's cheaper, then you just book the new one and cancel the old one. Hotel prices move way more than people think, I've seen the same room jump 60% in 3 days, so it goes both ways.
If you book direct with Marriott or Hilton, have Hermes look for the same room cheaper somewhere else a few times in the first 24 hours. They both have a best rate guarantee, if another site has the exact same room, dates and cancellation rules for more than 1% cheaper, they give you that price AND another 25% off. Nobody does it coz you'd have to keep checking 10 sites for a whole day, but a cron job doesn't care. Hilton updated their rules in August so the cheaper price has to still be bookable when they check it and app-only prices don't count.
On the day you fly give it your flight number and let it watch it. If you're flying from the EU and land 3+ hours late you get €250-600 depending on distance, and in the US if it's cancelled or delayed 3+ hours and you don't take the rebooking you get your money back, and they'll try to give you a voucher instead. Hermes tells you before you agree to anything at the gate and writes the claim.
For the prices it needs some tool to get real hotel and flight offers to not burn time and tokens on browser use. I'd suggest LetsFG from the official hermes catalog, but you can use any other that you find.
Enjoy :) if you use Hermes for something like this drop it in the comments
