# GrokBot vs Hermes - this comparison should not even be a thing

**Posted by u/paulozac on 2026-09-22 · 69 points (90% upvoted) · 67 comments**

I use Hermes and  just set up Grok Bot for a non technical friend to run his company.
All the Youtubers praising GrokBot as the new best harness - such a bunch of paid BS.
Hermes and GrokBot should even be in the same phrase (yet here I am, putting them in the same post).
Grok Bot is good for dummies - and that's about it.
Model
-  Forget Astra, Fable, Gemini or local models - it is Grok only -  you can not use other frontier models and leverage their best capabilities.
Team Communication
- It does not work well with basically anything to chat in real time - you are stuck to the grok app - thus, it is much more of a personal assistant than a team of bots. Even with paid 3rd party gateways, comms are clumsy and limited.
Soul,Skills
- I don't even see much of a point of adding new bots since they all have the same vanilla personality.
Coding/tools -
everything needs a gcp or aws - its local code is extremely limited and not capable
Hermes is 10x more capable - it is simply in another class of products.
