# Am I crazy to use yolo on everything?

**Posted by u/apalumbo on 2026-09-30 · 9 points (100% upvoted) · 28 comments**

I need a reality check because YouTube disclaimers and ChatGPT’s overly cautious approach says to sandbox this and isolate that. I don’t fully understand it, but the risks still exist in Docker. True isolation starts to look super cumbersome like having to manually feed it files I don’t mind it digesting. I can’t keep asking AI who agrees no matter what, I need the community’s actual take.
So I don’t really sandbox anything. Anyone else?
I run frequent backups of data.
I use only verified, or popular well supported MCPs and other open source projects.
This isn’t a discussion on local vs subscription models. Let’s assume all local models.
Do I really need to be concerned? People who feed it personal data, are you really dedicating a specific “shared folder” or “shared user” to isolate Hermes from everything and having to always specifically decide what is safe to have Hermes digest vs not?
The whole point of (my) Hermes is to be autonomous and not have to verify or monitor what it’s doing to accomplish the task at hand.
Common issue I run into, I set Xcode MCP up, had it working great, then something broke with the betas and it got very difficult to resolve. With Hermes on the Mac, it solved it immediately.
