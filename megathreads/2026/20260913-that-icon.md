# That icon.

**Posted by u/JPLamour on 2026-09-13 · 124 points (70% upvoted) · 144 comments**

This won’t be popular, but Hermes really needs to change their icon. It’s stupid, but such a seemingly insignificant detail like that hurts wider deployment in a business setting. It shouldn’t, but it does.
Getting the sign off from a non-technical decision maker to “trust me and this agentic harness in your org… just ignore the young anime girl that I will be placing in your team’s docks next to Slack, Excel, and Chrome” is a conversation I dread having as a consultant trying to build trust with a client.
Even just an option to change it during initial setup would be so much better. I don’t want to fork the project over a dopey icon.
