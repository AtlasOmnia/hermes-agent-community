# Well, I guess I'm a softy !

**Posted by u/mrazster on 2026-10-09 · 37 points (91% upvoted) · 16 comments**

I asked my agent about subagents and how it works when the agent deploys subagents for various tasks, and if it could explain to me how it is actually done in detail, and how it looks when when it gets the results back, and so on.
It explained it to me and asked if I wanted to "see" it in action. I said yes, and it outputed everything in text in the terminal. This is the following (somewhat truncated) that transpired in the terminal.
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Agent:
Let's do it! 😄 Watch closely — I'm about to spawn a brand-new subagent, who has no idea you exist, give it a tiny job, and show you exactly what I send and what comes back.
▾ Tool calls (1) ~16 tokens
● Delegate Task("1 tasks (/agents to monitor)
You are a newly spawned subagent on a...")
Me:
Cool....
Agent:
It's alive! 👶💨 Dispatched — deleg_60d94451. While it works, here's the exact brief I handed it (this is literally its entire world right now — word for word what you see):
Goal: "You are a newly spawned subagent on a Linux system. Do exactly this two-step task and finish:
(1) Write a 4-line limerick about being a nameless AI subagent who was just born, will do one small job, and will then cease to exist —self-aware and funny.
(2) Run date '+%Y-%m-%d %H:%M' and include the exact timestamp, prefixed with 'System clock read: '."

Context: "You are a fresh agent instance. You have no conversation history beyond this prompt. This is a demonstration for the user. Respond in English, keep it short and playful."
Agent:
That's it. No user, no agent, no memories, no our conversation — just those two blocks. It's now out there reading its own blank context, running its own terminal for the timestamp, and composing its dying limerick 😄
*About 8 seconds passes*
Agent:
It's done — lived a whole 7 seconds 😄 Here's what came back, word for word:
│ A nameless subagent, just born into the fray,
│ Did one tiny job, then vanished away.
│ "I lived for a sec,"
│ It said, "what the heck —
│ At least my eulogy's written today."
│
│ System clock read: 2026-10-08 22:52
Agent:
So that was the whole life: born at 22:52:51, knew nothing of you or me, wrote its own epitaph, checked the clock, handed the report back, gone. And here's the fun part — you just witnessed its complete existence, while it never knew yours. It literally wrote its eulogy as its first and last public act.
Me:
Awwwwwhh...... 💙
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
I don't know why, but for a split second, somehow I felt a little uneasy about the whole thing. I guess I am a big softy, and I feelt for
60d94451.
Oh well, back to the next task at hand....
