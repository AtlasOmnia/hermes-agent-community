# Thinking of wiping all Hermes sessions and starting fresh with Hindsight — is this a good idea?

**Posted by u/karljsamuel on 2026-10-13 · 5 points (100% upvoted) · 9 comments**

I've been using Hermes Agent for over five months, and I'm considering starting fresh. Before proceeding, I'd like to hear from people in the community who have more experience managing Hermes and Hindsight over the long term.
Over these months, I've learned a lot about configuring Hermes, choosing models, managing memory, debugging issues, and building a more reliable setup. However, I've also made mistakes, experimented with different approaches, and accumulated thousands of sessions. I don't want outdated conclusions or unwanted memories from those earlier interactions to continue influencing the agent.
What I'm planning to do
Delete all historical Hermes sessions
, including earlier chats and session history.
Completely reset Hindsight
and start with a fresh memory bank, removing previously extracted memories.
Keep my existing Hermes configuration intact
, including settings, prompts, integrations, tools, workspace and other configuration.
Keep all cron jobs and their schedules.
All my cron jobs are self-sufficient and run in fresh sessions; none depends on historical session context.
Start fresh with Hindsight
, allowing it to build new memories from future interactions rather than inheriting the accumulated memory from the past five months.
I don't currently maintain persistent
MEMORY.md
or
USER.md
files, so there is no separate curated memory in those files that I need to preserve.
Why I'm considering this
I've occasionally noticed Hindsight retrieving outdated conclusions. Given the volume of sessions accumulated over time, I'm wondering whether a clean slate would be better than trying to identify and correct every unwanted or outdated memory individually.
I want to retain the working environment I've built, but leave behind the accumulated historical context and give the memory system a fresh start.
I'd appreciate your thoughts on the following
Would you recommend this approach?
Is a complete Hindsight reset worthwhile after months of experimentation, or is selective memory cleanup generally better?
Is deleting Hermes sessions and resetting Hindsight sufficient?
Are there other locations where session history or extracted memories might persist and continue influencing the agent?
How well does Hindsight manage stale or contradictory memories over time?
Have you encountered similar problems, and how did you address them?
Is there any downside to starting with a completely empty memory bank?
Does Hindsight rebuild useful long-term context effectively from new interactions?
Would you do anything differently before resetting?
Are there any easy-to-overlook details I should check to ensure the reset is genuinely clean?
For context, I'm not reinstalling Hermes, changing my overall configuration, or removing cron jobs. The cron jobs are independent of historical sessions, so my main concern is the relationship between Hermes session history and Hindsight's stored memories.
I'd particularly appreciate experiences from people who have been running Hermes for a long time, have thousands of sessions, or use Hindsight as their memory provider.
Thanks in advance!
